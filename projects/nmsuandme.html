APP-06

NMSUandMe

A student networking platform that helps NMSU students meet through shared interests, mutual matching, and event-based meetups. Profiles are ranked by shared interests and major.

My part
Matching algorithm and backend
Team
Team project
Stack
Python, Flask, SQLAlchemy, SQLite, Vue

How it works

Students sign up with an NMSU email and build a profile around their major and interests. Profiles show up in order of how much you have in common with someone, which makes starting a conversation less awkward. Discovery uses a familiar swipe-style card stack.

My part

  • Built the vector-based similarity algorithm: profiles become vectors from attributes like major and interests, then get ranked by similarity.
  • Built the Flask and SQLAlchemy backend that serves those recommendations over REST.

Features

  • Interest-based matching. Recommendations ranked by profile similarity.
  • Swipe-style discovery. A card stack that makes browsing fast.
  • Mutual matching. Chat unlocks only after both people match.
  • Shared events. Create an event, invite connections, and accepted invites land on both calendars.

Architecture

  • Vue single-page app for profiles, discovery, and chat.
  • Python services for auth, matching, messaging, and events.
  • SQLite storage for users, matches, messages, and events.

What I learned

  • Turning a social idea into concrete data models and an algorithm.
  • Designing multi-step flows that balance discovery, consent, and communication.
  • Keeping a team's features consistent with clear interfaces, version control, and issue tracking.
  • How much a small logic error in the matching can affect the user experience.
